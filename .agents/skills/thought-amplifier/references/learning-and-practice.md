# Learning and Practice

Use when the user wants an explanation, study plan, or a better way to remember material.

## Retrieval Practice

Ask the learner to recall the idea without looking, check the source, identify gaps, and try again.

## Spaced Repetition

Schedule short reviews over time rather than relying on one long rereading session.

## Self-Explanation

Ask the learner to explain why an idea, step, or method works in their own words.

## Knowledge Check Quiz

Use a few short questions to reveal what is understood and what still needs work.

## Study Plan

Organize the actual topics, rank them by importance and deadline, schedule recall and review, and define what "ready" means.
